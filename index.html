03 / What we found + what we would fix

MAKE IT EASIER
TO CHOOSE YOU.One issue. One clear fix.

Each finding below explains what we saw, why it could get in a customer’s way, and the practical change we would make.

Finding 01 / 06

The policy links do not open policy pages.

What we found

Privacy Policy, Terms of Service and Accessibility in the implant-site footer each point back to the same page rather than to a dedicated policy page.

Why this can lose inquiries

A patient checking how their information will be handled cannot find the explanation they expected. That can undermine confidence just before they inquire.

Our fix

Connect the genuine, approved policy pages and check the same links beside the consultation form. Make the information easy to find before asking a patient to share details.

Proof: Footer link inspection ↗
Finding 02 / 06

The consultation form needs a reliability check.

What we found

The embedded form showed “refused to connect” in this cloud-browser session on the homepage and schedule page. This may be specific to the test environment; it does not prove the form fails for ordinary visitors.

Why this can lose inquiries

If the same issue appears on a patient’s device, an interested person cannot send the request. A visible fallback gives them another way to contact the practice.

Our fix

Test the form on normal phones and desktop browsers, check the form provider’s settings and confirm that requests reach the team. Keep a clear phone or alternative contact option available.

Proof: Observed browser limitation ↗
Finding 03 / 06

Patients need to know which office the reviews describe.

What we found

The implant homepage displays a combined five-star-review claim, without nearby details showing the location, platform or date behind that total.

Why this can lose inquiries

Someone choosing Roseville may want to check stories from Roseville. A practice-wide number alone makes that harder.

Our fix

Show source-linked reviews by office and label the date and platform. Keep practice-wide proof clearly separate from the proof for the selected location.

Proof: Homepage proof block ↗
Finding 04 / 06

Scheduling starts with a callback.

What we found

The schedule page says a coordinator calls or texts to find a time. The page is a consultation request, not a live choice of confirmed appointments.

Why this can lose inquiries

A patient may leave expecting a booked visit when the next step is actually a conversation. Clear expectations can reduce confusion and missed follow-up.

Our fix

Use “Request a consultation,” explain the callback step and show a response expectation the team can support. Track requests, confirmed consultations and attendance as different steps.

Proof: How scheduling works ↗
Finding 05 / 06

Credentials and warranty claims need details nearby.

What we found

The implant homepage uses a board-certified implant-specialist label, broad comparisons and warranty language. Supporting details are not presented beside those claims.

Why this can lose inquiries

A patient making a major treatment decision needs to understand the credential and what any warranty covers. Unexplained claims can make a reassuring message harder to evaluate.

Our fix

Confirm the credential source and warranty terms with the practice. Link or explain the supporting details, use specific statements the practice can substantiate and obtain qualified review of the wording.

Proof: Homepage credential / warranty copy ↗
Finding 06 / 06

The implant page sends a lot of data to a phone.

What we found

Google’s October 20 mobile lab test scored 54/100. The largest visible element appeared after 12.6 seconds, and the page transferred about 13 MB in that run. No real-user dataset was available; the test is not every patient’s experience.

Why this can lose inquiries

Heavy images and media can delay the useful information after an ad click, especially on a slower connection. Patients may leave before reaching the consultation step.

Our fix

Resize and compress the first-view images, show lightweight video cover images and load secondary players only when needed. Retest the same page and verify the consultation route after changes.

Proof: Mobile PageSpeed report ↗
