What we found / 1 of 3

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

01

Connect ads to consultations.

The separately hosted form needs a check linking ad clicks, requests and booked consultations. Otherwise, busy campaigns may not reveal which ads bring patients. Missing tracking is not established. See the consultation route ↗

02

12.6 seconds in the phone test.

October 20’s slow 4G homepage phone simulation also flagged heavy images. Potential implant patients can leave before seeing your stories or request form. Lab LCP, not every visit; no real-visitor data. See the phone test ↗

03

Policy links stop at the page.

Footer Privacy, Terms and Accessibility links all point to #. Patients cannot use these links to check how their information will be handled before sending a request. See the footer links ↗

What we found / 2 of 3

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

04

The form needs a reliable fallback.

The embedded form did not load in our cloud-browser test. If a patient encounters that problem, a ready inquiry stops at the final step. Ordinary-device failure remains unverified. See the request page ↗

05

The review number lacks context.

The combined five-star claim has no nearby office, platform or date breakdown. Patients comparing it with one location may doubt valuable proof. The total is not established as false. See the review claim ↗

06

A request is not a booked time.

Scheduling sends a request; a coordinator calls or texts to find a time. Patients expecting instant booking may miss follow-up or call elsewhere. See how scheduling works ↗

What we found / 3 of 3

FINDINGS. Worth fixing.

07

Big claims need specific support.

Board-certified implant-specialist, comparison and warranty claims lack nearby explanations. Unclear credentials or terms can turn strong claims into reasons to hesitate. No legal or clinical violation is concluded. See the claims ↗

08

Clarify each office’s contact route.

The implant site uses a central number; main-site contacts are office-specific. If campaign location and handoff differ, patients may reach the wrong route. Routing may be deliberate; confirm before changing. See the office contacts ↗

How we would help / 1 of 2

HERE’S THE FIX. The work we’d do.

01

Measure consultations safely.

Check site and form tracking with approved access. Report aggregate calls, requests, consultations and attendance, without sending patient or treatment details to ad platforms.

02

Make the first view lighter.

Resize and compress images, use lightweight video posters and load secondary embeds on demand. Retest the same phone profile while preserving the consultation route.

03 + 04

Protect the final inquiry step.

Connect approved policy pages. Reproduce the form test on ordinary phones and desktops, confirm embed settings and provide a dependable call or request fallback.

How we would help / 2 of 2

HERE’S THE FIX. The work we’d do.

05 + 07

Show proof patients can verify.

Label review sources, office scope and date. Explain verified credentials and warranty terms. Recut permission-cleared patient stories with honest individual context.

06 + 08

Make the handoff clear for each office.

Use request language, explain the callback and confirm office routing. Match each campaign’s location and treatment to its page, phone path and coordinator follow-up.
